Boomlet Support

Boomlet is a single-tap chain-reaction puzzle game by Uğur Özcan.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and suggestions: uguro9319@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model and the level number.

Common questions

  • How do I play? You get one tap per level. Pop everything on the board in a single chain reaction.
  • I changed phones. Where is "No Ads"? Open Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • Where is my progress stored? On your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refund requests go through their stores.
