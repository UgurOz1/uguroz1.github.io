Boomlet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Boomlet ("the game", "we") is a single-tap chain-reaction puzzle game for iPhone and Android, published by Uğur Özcan, an individual developer in Türkiye. This page explains what data the game and the services it uses handle, why, and what choices you have.

1. Short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device. We run no server of our own that stores your data.
  • The game uses third-party services for ads, analytics, crash reports, leaderboards and purchases. They process the data described below.
  • Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads or tracking you.

2. Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, scores, coins, lives, items, your settings (language, sound, vibration) and the purchases you have made are saved in a file on your device. We cannot see this file. It is removed when you uninstall the game. Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include it if you have backups turned on.

3. Services used by the game and the data they process

  • Advertising: Google AdMob (Google LLC), including ad networks that take part through AdMob mediation, for example Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and AppLovin. They may process your advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), IP address (approximate location), device model, operating system, language, and ad interactions, to deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent where required.
  • Analytics and crash reports: Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). They process app-instance identifiers, device and operating system information, app version, in-game events (for example the level you played, whether you passed it, which items you used), purchase and ad events, and crash logs. We use this to find bugs and to balance the levels.
  • Leaderboard: Apple Game Center (iPhone) and Google Play Games Services (Android). If you are signed in to the service on your device, your player name, player ID and score are sent to it so that the ranking can be shown. These services are run by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies. You can stay out of the leaderboard by not signing in, or by signing out in your device settings.
  • Purchases: Apple App Store and Google Play. Payments are handled completely by the store. We never receive your card or payment details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, the product and a transaction identifier.
  • Consent tools: Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) in regions where the law requires it, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on iPhone.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Why we use data and our legal basis

  • To run the game, keep your progress and process purchases: performance of the service you asked for.
  • To fix crashes, prevent fraud and understand how levels are played: our legitimate interest in a stable and fair game.
  • To show personalised ads and to track across other apps and sites: your consent. You can say no and still play the whole game; you will see non-personalised ads instead.

5. Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions the game asks for your choice when it first starts. You can change it any time in the game under Settings, Privacy options.
  • iPhone: you can allow or refuse tracking in the prompt, or later in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Refusing does not limit the game.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in Settings, Google, Ads.
  • Ads removal: an in-app purchase turns off the ads that appear between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay optional.

6. Retention

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period according to the retention settings of Firebase. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies. Purchase records are kept by Apple and Google.

7. International transfers

The services above are provided by companies in the United States and elsewhere, so your data can be processed outside your country. They rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. Children

Boomlet is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over (16 and over in the EEA where required). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given data through the game, write to us and we will help to have it removed.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK, or California CCPA) you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and withdraw consent. Because we hold no account data, most of the data about you sits with the services listed in section 3, which you can also contact directly. To exercise a right with us, write to the address below; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. For important changes we will tell you in the game.
